01

Email spoofing

SPF, DMARC, and DKIM decide whether someone can send email pretending to be you. Weak settings are how phishing lands in your customers' inboxes.

02

Known vulnerabilities

We match your server's software against public CVE databases and flag the ones attackers are actively exploiting right now.

03

Open doors

Databases, remote desktop, and admin ports left open to the whole internet. These are the doors ransomware walks through.

04

Web hardening

Missing security headers, leaked software versions, insecure cookies, old TLS, and pages that never switch to HTTPS.

05

Leaked files

The mistakes that hand over the keys: a public .git folder, an .env full of passwords, a database dump left behind.

06

Subdomain takeover

Old subdomains still pointing at deleted cloud services, which someone else could claim and use under your name.
